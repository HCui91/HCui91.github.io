ATLAS Forward Proton Δz Trigger Algorithm

ATLAS Experiment · CERN · 2022–2026 · merged into Athena and validated end to end

Developed an online trigger selection for the ATLAS Forward Proton (AFP) detector. It compares the longitudinal vertex position measured by the AFP time-of-flight detectors with the one reconstructed by the inner detector, and rejects forward protons that came from a different interaction than the central event. Its two working points, 5 mm and 10 mm, reject uncorrelated proton–vertex combinations by factors of about ten and five.

Contribution: design and implementation of the HLT hypothesis algorithm and tool, their monitoring, migration of the AFP chains to the component-accumulator configuration, and end-to-end validation in two Athena releases. The selection was not enabled for Run 3 data taking.

Flavour-Resolved Lund Jet Plane in tt Events

ATLAS Experiment · CERN · full Run 2 dataset, 140 fb⁻¹ at √s = 13 TeV

A measurement of the Lund jet plane resolved by jet flavour (b-, c- and light-jets), the first of its kind at ATLAS. GN2v01 track-origin labelling separates heavy-flavour decay products from QCD radiation, and the ratios of heavy-flavour to light-jet planes are built to isolate the dead-cone effect: the suppression of collinear radiation around heavy quarks.

Covers R = 0.4 jets with 20 < pT < 200 GeV, a range below the two existing ATLAS Lund-jet-plane measurements, as a test of parton-shower and hadronization models in the heavy-flavour sector. Results will appear in my thesis and an ATLAS paper.

Contribution: derivation development, track-origin labelling and flavour tagging, and the unfolding setup (OmniFold-based, iterative, with closure tests).
